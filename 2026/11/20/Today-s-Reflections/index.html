A Small Wish for a Happy Family

These days I’m living with my parents. I’ve been watching how they communicate with each other, hoping to find a glimpse of a bright future in their everyday life.

But that isn’t possible. It’s foolish to look for my future in someone else’s life.

My mother has many rules at home. She wants my father and me to follow them: hold your bowl while eating breakfast, lunch, or dinner; never shake your legs; drink a glass of water first thing every morning.

She does almost all the housework, and that’s probably why she believes she deserves our respect and obedience. Today my father tried to do something for the family by bringing home some fish for 100 yuan. That made my mother very unhappy. She kept complaining that the fish wasn’t fresh enough and that the shop wasn’t a good one.

I can’t understand her, and I really don’t know what I can do. I want a happy family: after work each day, we could sit together, eat together, and talk together. After dinner, we could even take a short walk around the park.

Maybe I’m asking for too much. Maybe I should change my mind and find a new way to live with my parents. I should keep myself under control and stay happy.

I don’t know if AI can help me find a solution.
